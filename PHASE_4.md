# GK Advisory — Phase 4

## Lead Generation & Commercial Intake

Phase 4 converts the website contact journey into a structured advisory intake flow.

### Delivered
- Dedicated `contact.html` diagnostic request page.
- Structured fields for person, company, role, company size, service and problem statement.
- Consent checkbox.
- Demo lead persistence through browser `localStorage`.
- Initial lead scoring: HOT / WARM.
- `leads.html` pipeline view for prototype review.
- Main website CTA updated from generic email to diagnostic request.

### Architecture note
This is intentionally a front-end prototype. Before production, replace localStorage with a secure backend/API, database, authenticated admin access, audit logging, consent records, rate limiting and GDPR/KVKK-compliant retention controls.

## CFO-level commercial KPI model

Recommended future KPIs: inquiry-to-meeting conversion, meeting-to-proposal conversion, proposal win rate, average project value, sales cycle days, recurring revenue share and pipeline coverage.
